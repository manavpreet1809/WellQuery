Before importing code:

1. Confirm whether the project owner wrote it, collaborated on it, or obtained it from another author.

3. Confirm permission to reuse and redistribute it, and preserve required license and copyright notices.
4. Check course requirements for prior work and third-party code, and clearly distinguish reused components from new contributions.

Document-source permissions and attribution will be recorded separately when the knowledge base is selected. Permission to reuse application code does not establish permission to reuse its source documents.
